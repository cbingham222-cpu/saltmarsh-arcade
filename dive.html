A Saltmarsh arcade

Crabber's Cove Dive

One breath, one dive. The deeper the cove, the richer the wrecks, but you only keep what you surface with.

Best on this device

    Tap left or right of the diver to change lanes. Also in the arcade: Weatherlight Run

    The crabbers' rules

    How to dive

    One breath

    You start beside the skiff and sink head-first into the cove. Tap left or right of the diver to slide one lane that way (arrow keys or A and D on a keyboard). Esc or P pauses.

    When you've got enough, tap Surface (or press ↑, W or Space) to turn around and kick for the air. There's no going back down. The way up passes the same rocks and nets you dodged on the way down, and the sahuagin follow you up.

    Breath

    The bar top right drains every second, and faster the deeper you are. The gold tick on it is the breath you need to swim straight up from here without a hit. The bar turns gold when you're cutting it close and red when you won't make it without an air bubble. Run out and you drown, and everything you were carrying stays in the cove.

    In the cove

    Rocks. They sit still. Hitting one costs 6 seconds of breath.

    Crab nets. From 12 fathoms down. They sway between two lanes, snag you for a moment and cost 3 seconds of breath.

    Sahuagin. From 30 fathoms down. A green ripple shows where one lurks, then it darts at you. Getting caught steals 30% of the gold you carry and 3 seconds of breath.

    Loot

    Coins. 10 gold each, often in lines of three.

    Pearls. Below 20 fathoms. 40 gold.

    Chests. Below 50 fathoms, and rare. 150 gold.

    Air bubbles. 8 seconds of breath back.

    Loot is worth more the deeper you find it: double at 25 fathoms, triple at 50, five times at 100. Every two pieces in a row raise your combo by one, up to ×8, and any hit drops it back to ×1.

    The dark

    Your lantern reaches less far the deeper you go. Pearls and chests glint in the dark, and you can always see a sahuagin's ripple.

    Scoring

    Surface to bank everything you're carrying, plus 10 gold for every fathom of your deepest point. Drown and you bank nothing.
